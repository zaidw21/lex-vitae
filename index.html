Firm overview

Lex Vitae Solicitors and Consultants

Lex Vitae Legal Services is a complete legal solutions firm from Central India, with its office at Indore and associate offices at Bhopal, Nagpur, Mumbai and Delhi.

Established by alumni of National Law University, the firm aims to provide professional and ethical legal services to its clients. We work towards developing successful legal strategies through the optimum use of our skills and ethical conduct, to give the best available legal support to the system, the courts and our clients.

We offer comprehensive legal services through our own office and associate offices in Maharashtra, Delhi, Karnataka, Punjab, Haryana and Chhattisgarh, as well as surrounding areas.

How we work

A client-first approach

We work closely with each client to understand their circumstances, communicate clearly and keep them updated throughout the legal process, from first consultation to final outcome.

  1. Case analysis

    We listen, review your documents and identify the legal issues and the options open to you.

  2. Research & investigation

    We build the facts and the law behind your matter and settle on a clear strategy.

  3. Representation

    We advocate for you in negotiation, arbitration or court, and keep you informed at every stage.
